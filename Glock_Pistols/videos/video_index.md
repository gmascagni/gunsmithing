# Glock Pistols — Curated Video Reference Index

Comprehensive video references for professional gunsmiths and certified armorers covering Glock Gen 1–5 pistols (Models 17, 19, 21, 22, 23, 26, 34, 43, 43X, 48).

## 1. Complete Detail Strip & Armorer Disassembly

### Glock Slide Detail Strip & Component Inspection
- **Channel / Creator:** Brownells / Smyrna Armorer Guides
- **Focus:** Complete disassembly of slide components: extractor, spring-loaded bearing, extractor depressor plunger, firing pin assembly (cups, striker spring, spacer sleeve), and firing pin safety plunger.
- **Key Inspection Points:** Firing pin channel liner condition, firing pin lug wear, extractor claw chip check.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYJjQn-B-Qc
- **Duration:** 12:45

### Glock Frame Detail Strip & Reassembly (Gen 3, 4 & 5 Differences)
- **Channel / Creator:** GlockStore / Lenny Magill
- **Focus:** Locking block pin (Gen 3/4) vs single pin (Gen 5), trigger pin, housing pin, trigger bar with cruciform, ejector housing, and connector removal.
- **Key Armorer Notes:** Proper punch order (top locking block pin first to relieve trigger pin pressure), slide stop lever spring seating beneath top pin.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oV0wDDFV0NY
- **Duration:** 18:20

## 2. Safety Inspections & Armorer Tolerances

### Glock 3-Stage "Safe Action" Armorer Bench Inspection
- **Channel / Creator:** Midwest Gun Works / Glock Factory Training
- **Focus:**
  1. **Trigger Safety:** Ensuring trigger cannot fire without lever depression.
  2. **Firing Pin Safety:** Plunger drop test and forward travel block test.
  3. **Drop Safety:** Cruciform drop shelf engagement verification using orange Armorer Inspection Plate.
- **Critical Spec:** Striker lug to cruciform sear surface must maintain **minimum 2/3 (66%) engagement** across all generations.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0f631Z8m08
- **Duration:** 14:15

### Connector Angle Tuning & Feeler Gauge Clearance
- **Channel / Creator:** Johnny Glocks (Master Glock Tuner)
- **Focus:** Adjusting the connector outward bend angle for crisp break and positive reset. Setting proper clearance between trigger bar and right frame wall.
- **Key Spec:** ~0.005" to 0.010" gap; connector must hold outward spring tension against trigger mechanism housing.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh_B14eQzT4
- **Duration:** 16:50

## 3. Custom Gunsmithing, Trigger Jobs & Tuning

### The Famous 25¢ Glock Trigger Polish Job
- **Channel / Creator:** MCORPS1 / Gunsmith Training
- **Focus:** Mirror polishing factory friction surfaces with Flitz or Mothers polish (sear shelf, cruciform nose, connector ramp, safety plunger dome, and trigger bar tab) without removing metal or altering factory sear angles.
- **Benefit:** Smooths out pre-travel grit, decreases perceived pull weight by 0.5–0.75 lbs while maintaining 100% factory safety reliability.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2mszC0g_yC4
- **Duration:** 15:30

### Glock Slide Rail Clearance & Lubrication Standards
- **Channel / Creator:** Larry Vickers / Vickers Tactical
- **Focus:** Factory lubrication discipline: exactly 5 drops of oil (1 drop slide rails, 1 drop barrel hood, 1 drop barrel exterior, 1 drop connector/trigger bar hook). Zero oil in the striker channel to prevent light primer strikes from hydraulicking oil/gunk.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGobEpUO3Uc
- **Duration:** 08:40
