## 2. Critical Inspections, Tolerances & Fatigue Checks

### Locking Block Inspection, Generation Identification & Replacement
- **Channel / Creator:** Wilson Combat Gunsmithing
- **Focus:** Identifying 1st Gen (square cuts, stress risers) vs 2nd/3rd Gen (radiused cuts, fatigue-resistant metallurgy). Inspecting locking block lugs/ears under magnification for micro-fractures; measuring plunger play and frame locking recesses.
- **Critical Spec:** Replace locking block and plunger assembly every 15,000–20,000 rounds; replace recoil spring every 3,000–5,000 rounds.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D-wHq_KzB_4
- **Duration:** 12:40

### Beretta 92FS Enlarged Hammer Pin Safety Inspection
- **Channel / Creator:** Small Arms Solutions / Chris Bartocci
- **Focus:** Engineering history and armorer inspection of the 92FS oversized hammer pin and slide channel groove, which stops rearward slide separation in the event of extreme overpressure slide failure.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-N8J8nL9k0
- **Duration:** 18:50

## 3. Custom Gunsmithing, Upgrades & Trigger Jobs

### Factory "D" Hammer Spring Conversion & Double-Action Tuning
- **Channel / Creator:** The Armory Channel
- **Focus:** Replacing standard civilian/military mainspring with Beretta 92D (DAO) spring. Drops double-action trigger pull from ~12 lbs down to a smooth, reliable ~8 lbs with 100% primer ignition reliability.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kHwFvLgT5s
- **Duration:** 09:20

### Installing Wilson Combat Short-Reach Trigger & Trigger Return Bar Unit
- **Channel / Creator:** Wilson Combat / Bill Wilson
- **Focus:** Upgrading standard trigger shoe to steel contoured short-reach profile and replacing coil mouse-trap trigger spring with encapsulated trigger return unit.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1uN7v8mFqLo
- **Duration:** 14:10

## 4. Troubleshooting & Malfunction Diagnoses

### Diagnosing Trigger Bar Spring Disengagement (Dead Trigger)
- **Channel / Creator:** Mosin Virus / Handgun Armorer
- **Focus:** When right grip panel is removed or loose, trigger bar spring pops out of its groove, causing the trigger to pull completely dead with no hammer cocking. Correct spring positioning and grip screw bushing inspection.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yY3kE1pU8bQ
- **Duration:** 07:45

### Beretta 92 Extraction & Ejection Malfunctions (FTE / Weak Ejection)
- **Channel / Creator:** Apex Gunsmithing
- **Focus:** Cleaning carbon behind extractor hook, measuring extractor spring tension with armorer pull gauge, and checking fixed ejector blade on left frame rail.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gX7wH_P9k8
