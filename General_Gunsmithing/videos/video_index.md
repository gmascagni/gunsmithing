# General Gunsmithing — Curated Video Reference Index

Masterclass video references for professional gunsmiths covering core workshop disciplines: headspace gauging, barrel work, chamber casting, metal finishing & rust bluing, action bedding, and precision sear stoning.

---

## 1. Headspace & Chamber Diagnostics

### Headspace Gauging Demystified: Go, No-Go & Field Gauges
- **Channel / Creator:** Mark Novak / Anvil Gunsmithing
- **Focus:** Complete theory and hands-on demonstration of headspace gauges. Differences across rimmed cartridges (.303 Brit, 7.62x54R, .30-30), rimless (.30-06, .308 Win, 5.56 NATO), and belted magnums.
- **Key Safety Rule:** Bolt must close on GO gauge without binding. Bolt must NOT close on NO-GO gauge with gentle finger pressure. If bolt closes on FIELD gauge, the firearm is unsafe to fire.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J98Fk7s2L_8
- **Duration:** 28:40

### Chamber Casting with Cerrosafe Low-Melt Alloy
- **Channel / Creator:** Mark Novak / Anvil Gunsmithing
- **Focus:** Casting an unknown or worn rifle/shotgun chamber using Cerrosafe alloy (melts at $160^\circ\text{F} - 190^\circ\text{F}$). Plugging the bore, pre-heating the barrel, pouring, and cooling.
- **Crucial Timing Window:** Cerrosafe shrinks during initial cooling; cast measures exact chamber dimensions at **exactly 1 hour after pouring** before secondary expansion begins.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vV1p8_oG9_Q
- **Duration:** 22:15

## 2. Metal Finishing & Restoration

### Traditional Slow Rust Bluing: Chemistry, Rust Box & Carding
- **Channel / Creator:** Mark Novak / Anvil Gunsmithing
- **Focus:** Traditional slow-rust bluing process. Degreasing with acetone/lye, applying acid rusting solution, sweating in humidity cabinet, boiling in distilled water to convert red ferric oxide ($\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$) to deep black ferrosoferric oxide ($\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4$ / magnetite), and carding with fine $0.003''$ wire wheel.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48S-1jJ9t0k
- **Duration:** 35:20

### Hot Caustic Salt Bluing Setup, Chemistry & Safety
- **Channel / Creator:** MidwayUSA / Larry Potterfield
- **Focus:** Operating hot bluing tanks at $285^\circ\text{F} - 292^\circ\text{F}$ using sodium hydroxide and sodium nitrate salts. Tank sequence (degreaser, acid etch, hot caustic bluing, cold rinse, boiling rinse, water-displacing oil).
- **Safety Alert:** Temperature control and boil-over mitigation (never add cold water directly to boiling salts).
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kY0R3M_P-58
- **Duration:** 19:45

## 3. Barrel & Action Precision Work

### Cutting an 11-Degree Target Barrel Crown
- **Channel / Creator:** MidwayUSA / Larry Potterfield
- **Focus:** Facing and crowning a rifle muzzle on a lathe or with a Manson hand crowning tool and brass pilots. Ensuring concentricity with the bore axis to prevent gas turbulence as the bullet base exits the rifling.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eG_4kM_x900
- **Duration:** 14:10

### Rifle Action Glass Bedding & Pillar Bedding
- **Channel / Creator:** Brownells Gun Techs
- **Focus:** Stress-free action bedding using Brownells Acraglas Gel and aluminum pillars. Stock hogging, clay dam construction, applying PVA release agent (preventing the action from being permanently glued into stock), and torquing during cure.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VwK9T_P22o4
- **Duration:** 24:30

## 4. Trigger Sears & Fastener Tooling

### Precision Trigger Sear Stoning with True-Angle Jigs
- **Channel / Creator:** MidwayUSA / Larry Potterfield
- **Focus:** Using Norton hard Arkansas oil stones and precision sear fixture jigs. Removing machine marks and creep without changing the critical primary engagement angle ($90^\circ$) or rounding the razor-sharp engagement nose.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqN6s9O8Pz8
- **Duration:** 17:15

### Custom Grinding Screwdrivers for Gunsmith Fasteners
- **Channel / Creator:** Brownells Gun Techs
- **Focus:** Why standard wedge-shaped hardware screwdrivers destroy gun screws ("cam-out" and burred slots). Grinding parallel hollow-ground driver tips to fill the entire slot width and depth cleanly.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yW6eL8Bq96E
- **Duration:** 12:10
