⚠️ CRITICAL WORKSHOP SAFETY: RETURN MAINSPRING

The return spring is a flat spiral clock spring inside a circular gear casing. NEVER remove the trigger group or gear casing without first easing spring tension under control using the tension key or holding the pinion teeth. Uncontrolled release will shear the pinion teeth and fracture the spring arbor.

Spring Tension & Pinion Calibration

12 to 14 LBS PULL

Measured at the cocking handle as it initiates rearward stroke. Standard baseline from complete slack is 2 to 2½ full revolutions of the gear casing.

Striker (Firing Pin) Protrusion

0.050" to 0.060"

Striker is fixed directly to the vertical post of the operating rod. Protrusion through the bolt face must be verified with the bolt locked in full battery.

Gas Regulator Ports

'S' (Small) / 'L' (Large)

Rotate regulator cup to 'S' for clean weapon in normal service; rotate to 'L' to overcome heavy carbon fouling, freezing temperatures, or sandy field conditions.

Headspace Tolerances (.303 British)

0.058" GO / 0.064" NO-GO

Gauged from the barrel breech face to the bolt recess face. Maximum allowable FIELD headspace is 0.070".

Immediate Action: 4-Position Stoppage System

Cocking Handle Position Diagnosis Armorer & Shop Remedy
Position 1 (Fully Forward) Misfire, dud cartridge, empty pan, or worn striker. Recock; check pan feed pawls; verify striker protrusion (min 0.050").
Position 2 (1"–2" Mid-Stroke) Short recoil; weak return spring; heavy carbon in gas cylinder. Increase spring tension 1–2 clicks; scrape carbon from gas cup and cylinder.
Position 3 (Near Full Rear) Failure to extract or eject; ruptured cartridge case in chamber. Inspect extractor claws and ejector pivot; use broken shell extractor for separated cases.
Position 4 (Jammed Full Rear) Mechanical bind; broken clock spring; sheared rack/pinion tooth. Strip gear casing; check mainspring; roll piston rod on flat to verify straightness.
