# Ruger Firearms — Curated Video Reference Index

Comprehensive video references for professional gunsmiths covering the **Ruger 10/22 Carbine**, **Ruger Mark I / II / III / IV Target Pistols**, and **Ruger GP100 Double-Action Revolvers**.

## 1. Ruger 10/22 Carbine Workshop Series

### Complete Detail Disassembly & Modular Trigger Group Strip
- **Channel / Creator:** MidwayUSA / Larry Potterfield
- **Focus:** Removing receiver from stock, takedown screw torque, removing trigger group pins, bolt buffer pin, bolt removal, charging handle guide spring, and trigger group detail strip (sear, disconnector, hammer spring strut).
- **Key Armorer Notes:** Direction of hammer strut washer notch (open slot facing upwards).
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3aJ2Y4m0Xo
- **Duration:** 14:40

### 10/22 Bolt Tuning: Extractor Upgrade & Firing Pin Staking
- **Channel / Creator:** Brownells Gun Techs
- **Focus:** Replacing factory stamped extractor with wire-EDM hardened sharp-hook extractor (Volquartsen / Kidd), smoothing bolt face contact, and testing headspace on rim recess ($0.0425'' - 0.043''$).
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RkLq7oUaF88
- **Duration:** 10:25

### Rotary Magazine Disassembly, Tensioning & Cleaning
- **Channel / Creator:** GunBlue490
- **Focus:** Disassembling the legendary 10/22 10-round rotary magazine. Pre-loading the rotor clock spring by exactly 1-1/4 turns ($450^\circ$) before cinching the central allen nut.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wX-yV2kP7q0
- **Duration:** 16:15

## 2. Ruger Mark I / II / III / IV Target Pistols

### Mastering the Ruger Mark I / II / III Takedown & The Dreaded Reassembly
- **Channel / Creator:** GunBlue490
- **Focus:** Step-by-step masterclass demystifying the Ruger Mark series reassembly puzzle.
- **Key Crucial Trick:** Gravity alignment of the hammer strut. Pointing the muzzle $45^\circ$ upward to drop hammer forward, pointing muzzle up to seat hammer strut into the mainspring housing cup before closing the latch.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b4Owh_qH_5k
- **Duration:** 22:30

### Ruger Mark III Magazine Disconnect Removal & Bushing Mod
- **Channel / Creator:** Tandemkross Gunsmith Tutorials
- **Focus:** Eliminating the factory magazine safety disconnect using a Sam Lam or Tandemkross hammer bushing. Enables magazines to drop free cleanly and simplifies detail stripping down to Mark II simplicity.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6P7K_tVf28M
- **Duration:** 11:50

### Ruger Mark IV Push-Button Takedown & Internal Overhaul
- **Channel / Creator:** Volquartsen Firearms
- **Focus:** Complete overview of the redesigned one-button hinge takedown system on Mark IV and installation of target accurizing trigger kits.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhL0L_yD4_c
- **Duration:** 13:45

## 3. Ruger GP100 Double-Action Revolvers

### Ruger GP100 Complete Detail Disassembly & Subassembly Modular Strip
- **Channel / Creator:** GunBlue490
- **Focus:** Modular construction breakdown: removing grip peg and mainspring strut, latching trigger guard latch pin, removing trigger assembly module, crane latch and cylinder assembly, and transfer bar safety.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u1j9qG-Z-84
- **Duration:** 25:10

### Revolver Timing, Cylinder Gap & Endshake Tuning
- **Channel / Creator:** MidwayUSA / Larry Potterfield
- **Focus:** Measuring barrel-cylinder gap with feeler gauge ($0.004'' - 0.007''$), measuring cylinder endplay/endshake ($<0.002''$), installing crane shims, and testing carry-up timing across all 6 chambers.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8V-w6Qz7fK0
- **Duration:** 18:35

### GP100 Trigger Action Job & Wolff Spring Kit Installation
- **Channel / Creator:** The Armory Channel
- **Focus:** Stoning the trigger return slide channel, polishing hammer dog sear contact, and installing 10 lb hammer spring and 8 lb trigger return spring for a silky 8.5 lb double action / 2.75 lb single action.
- **Reference URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tF6v5Z8m_2A
- **Duration:** 20:15
