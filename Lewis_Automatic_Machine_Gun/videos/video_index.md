## 2. Disassembly, Assembly & Field Stripping

### [Project Lightening Episode 04: Field Strip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I3ZA9rg8uKI)
* **Channel:** C&Rsenal & Forgotten Weapons (Othais & Ian McCollum)
* **Focus:** Practical, step-by-step takedown of the Lewis Gun with real vintage military examples.
* **Key Workshop Sequence:**
  1. **Clearing & Safety:** Verify feedway and chamber empty, cocking handle forward.
  2. **Buttstock Removal:** Depressing the stock latch and sliding the buttstock assembly rearward and off the receiver.
  3. **Return Spring De-tensioning:** **CRITICAL SAFETY STEP** — Controlling the pinion and spiral mainspring (clock spring) tension before opening the trigger group.
  4. **Trigger Group Separation:** Dropping the pistol grip and trigger housing down and out.
  5. **Bolt & Operating Rod Extraction:** Drawing the operating rod, rack, and bolt assembly out through the rear of the receiver.
  6. **Feed Mechanism Cover:** Lifting the feed arm, feed cover, and feed pawl assembly.

### [Lewis Light Machine Gun - Step By Step Disassembly & Reassembly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mnp4J-5UNZI)
* **Focus:** Methodical tabletop bench guide for complete strip down and reassembly.
* **Key Points:**
  * Pin removal order to prevent binding or shear stress.
  * Handling the radiator casing, barrel clamp, and gas regulator cup.
  * Reassembly timing: aligning the piston rack teeth with the clock spring pinion gear.

## 3. 3D Mechanical Cycling & Internal Cutaway Animations

* **Gunsmith Utility:**

  * Highlighting the rotation of the 4-lug bolt inside the receiver guide rails.
  * Internal cutaway showing the spiral mainspring casing and ratchet mechanism.

### [How A Lewis Machine Gun Works | Animation Of The Mechanisms Of The Lewis LMG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TS9bR9EiRd8)
* **Focus:** Motion breakdown of the complete firing cycle:
  1. **Gas Tap:** High-pressure propellant gas ports through the barrel into the gas cylinder.
  2. **Piston Retraction:** Gas impinges on the cup-faced piston, driving the rack rearward.
  3. **Clock Spring Winding:** The piston rack rotates the pinion, winding the spiral spring.
  4. **Camming & Unlocking:** The post on the piston moves through the helical cam slot in the bolt, unlocking the 4 lugs.
  5. **Extraction & Ejection:** Extractor claws pull the spent casing against the pivoted ejector arm.
  6. **Feed Arm Oscillation:** The rearward motion of the bolt post pivots the feed cover arm, advancing the pan magazine by one cartridge tooth via feed pawls.
